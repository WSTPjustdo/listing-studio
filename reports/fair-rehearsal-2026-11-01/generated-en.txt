The THERMOS Stainless King 16oz Tumbler holds 16oz (475 ml) and is made with 18/8 stainless steel inside and out, with double-wall vacuum insulation. THERMOS states it keeps beverages hot for up to 7 hours and cold for up to 18 hours. The DrinkLock sealing lid is leak-proof when closed and properly assembled. A built-in tea hook holds tea bags or most loose leaf infusers, and the tumbler fits most standard car cup holders. Dishwasher safe, top rack only.
